Consider $S$ and $S'$ two shapes.
We say that $S$ is less coarse than $S'$ (or $S$ is more precise than $S'$) -- denoted $S <: S'$ -- if for all graphs $G$ we have the following:
If there are no violations for $S$ at $G$, then there are no violations of $S'$ at $G$.

# example

Consider our [artifical example](../samples-input/people-datetime.ttl) where 90% of people have `xsd:date` as `foaf:birthday` and 10% have `xsd:dateTime`.

We may consider the following shapes in order of decreasing precision (simplified, modulo minCount/maxCount, only mentioning what differs):

1. `foaf:birthday` should be a `xsd:date` AND `xsd:dateTime` (vacuously false; BUG)

    Generated by: `shexer(0,_)`

2. `foaf:birthday` should be a `xsd:date` OR `xsd:dateTime` (the most precise sound one)

    Generated by: `play`, `qse(0)`

3. `foaf:birthday` should be a `xsd:date`

    Generated by: `qse(0.5)`, `qse(0.9)`[*], `shexer(0.5,_)`, `shexer(0.9,_)`

4. `foaf:birthday` can be anything

    Generated by:`shexer(1,_)`, `voicl`, `qse(1)`[*]

[*] bug QSE; qse(0.9) actually generate shapes described by (4); possibly a bug in constraint cutoff; using $<\phi$ instead of $<=\phi$?
Similarly, qse(1) poses no constraints on `foaf:name`.
